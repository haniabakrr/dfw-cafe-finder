Cinnamon Toast Latte

Cinnamon-sugar toast in latte form: warm cinnamon and a sweet, toasty finish on La Colombe espresso.

Where: Funny Library Coffee Shop

Area: Design District, inside Virgin Hotels

Tip: Want it boozy? They can add Bailey's or Amaretto.

Crème Brûlée Latte

A latte inspired by the classic French dessert: vanilla custard flavor with a caramelized, burnt-sugar finish.

Where: Houndstooth Coffee

Area: 1900 N Henderson Ave, plus Walnut Hill and Sylvan Thirty

Tip: The Henderson shop closes at 2pm, so go early.

Biscoff Cheesecake Latte

Dessert in a cup: the spiced caramel taste of Biscoff cookies with creamy cheesecake, poured over espresso.

Where: Bloom Cafe

Area: 1815 Campbell Rd, Garland

Tip: If it's sold out, try their Lotus Biscoff Latte.

Elote Cold Foam

Cafe Ciro turns elote, Mexican street corn, into a salted purée and whips it into cold foam. Sweet, salty and a little savory on top of iced coffee.

Where: Cafe Ciro

Area: Pleasant Grove, on Buckner Blvd

Tip: Not sure about corn? Try the Bebeleche, a dulce de leche cold foam with pecans.

Toffee Brown Butter Bourbon Latte

Inspired Brew soaks its beans in whiskey for six weeks before roasting. The roasting burns off the alcohol but keeps the flavor, so the coffee is non-alcoholic. This latte adds toffee and brown butter on top.

Where: Inspired Brew

Area: 19009 Preston Rd, Plano

Tip: Ask them to print a picture on your foam.

Maple Cinnamon Roll Cold Brew

The flavor of a cinnamon roll with maple glaze, poured over cold brew.

Where: Herb's House Coffee + Company

Area: 5622 Dyer St #100, Dallas

Tip: Herb's is quiet, so order this and stay for a study session.

Cafe Olivia

A bright, beautiful café inside the Dallas Arts Tower. Everything is made from scratch with local ingredients, with lots of vegan and vegetarian options.

Address: 2200 Ross Ave #100, Dallas, TX 75201

Hours: Monday–Friday, 7am–3pm

Good for: Studying on weekday mornings

Try: The Olivia Latte or the Fig and Rose Matcha

Funny Library Coffee Shop

A playful take on a library, with shelves of whimsical books and coffee table reads. It doubles as a workspace, with communal tables and comfy couches.

Address: Virgin Hotels Dallas, 1445 Turtle Creek Blvd, Dallas, TX 75207

Hours: Every day, 7am–5pm

Wi-Fi: Free

Good for: Studying

Seasonal drink here: Cinnamon Toast Latte

Roché Coffee

Vietnamese coffee made the traditional way, with beans from Vietnam brewed through a metal phin. They also hand-whisk Japanese matcha for every order.

Address: 17062 Preston Rd #130, Dallas, TX

Good for: Hanging out with friends

Try: The Einspänner Latte, topped with Viennese whipped cream

← Back to all drinks and spots

Otto's Coffee & Fine Foods

Styled after old Viennese coffeehouses, with dark wood and marble tables, inside The Adolphus hotel. They serve locally roasted Full City Rooster coffee.

Address: The Adolphus, 1321 Commerce St, Dallas, TX

Hours: Every day, 6:30am–10pm

Good for: Studying late and hanging out

Tip: Street parking is hard to find, so use the hotel valet.

← Back to all drinks and spots

Buzz & Bustle Coffee + Bodega

Comfy couches, lots of indoor and outdoor seating, and live music nights. A fun spot to meet friends.

Address: 5610 Village Glen Dr, Dallas, TX

Good for: Hanging out, or studying in the morning when it's quiet

Tip: Free parking in the garage below.
